Whitepaper白皮书

1. What BNAGENT is

BNAGENT is a meme token on BNB Smart Chain with a fair, on-chain mint reserved for Binance Agentic Wallets holding a .ai Agent ID. It follows the $JAGENT (jager.live) mint mechanism. Supply only comes into existence when shares are minted. There is no presale, no private round and no team allocation.

2. Token

  • Name / symbol: BNAGENT / BNAGENT, 18 decimals, BEP-20.
  • Hard cap: 4,440,000,000,000 BNAGENT, enforced by the token. Only the mint contract can create tokens.
  • Buy tax 1% (converted to BNB for the treasury). Sell tax 1% burned + 1% converted to BNB for the treasury. Wallet-to-wallet transfers: no tax. Rates and recipient are fixed in code.
  • Until the pool opens, BNAGENT cannot be transferred. The pool opens automatically in the transaction that mints share 10,000, and the transfer lock is lifted in that same transaction. Afterwards only the official BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap pair is accepted as a pool: transfers into other pairs holding BNAGENT, the PancakeSwap V4 PoolManager and the Infinity Vault are refused. Routers can never be blocked.

3. Minting

  • 30,000 shares in total. Shares #1–#1,100 are burned at deploy: they are never sold, and no tokens or USDT exist for them. Selling starts at share #1,101, so 28,900 shares are for sale (46,300 USDT at table prices).
  • Eligibility: the sending wallet must hold a .ai Agent ID (read on-chain from the SPACE ID registry).
  • Quota: 10 shares per Agent ID, +1 per invited Agent ID's first paid mint, +1 per social task (3 tasks). Max 10 shares per transaction.
  • Shares #1,101–#10,000 cost 1 USDT and give 50,000,000 BNAGENT each. From share 10,001 the payment buys BNAGENT at the pool price minus 1%.
  • Pay with USDT (approve, then mint with a maximum cost) or with BNB (priced with Chainlink BNB/USD plus 1%, swapped to USDT on PancakeSwap in the same transaction; BNB above the quote is refunded).
  • Of every payment, 20% goes straight to the project treasury (0x49E6…9BCE) and 80% stays with the mint contract for liquidity. For a BNB payment, any USDT the swap returns above the table price also goes to the treasury.

4. Liquidity

  • Up to share 10,000, each share parks 0.80 USDT and 40,000,000 BNAGENT in the mint contract. The transaction that reaches share 10,000 opens the official BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap pool automatically with everything parked: 8,900 paid shares × 0.80 = 7,120 USDT and 356,000,000,000 BNAGENT (the same 1 USDT = 50,000,000 BNAGENT price) — or less, if the team has withdrawn under the 30-day rule: then only what was parked after its last withdrawal (section 5). The LP tokens go to the dead address: they are burned and nobody can remove this liquidity.
  • After that, 80% of each mint payment is added to the pool at the pool price, again with the LP burned.
  • Floor line: line = total USDT paid × 83,492 / 46,300 (≈ 1.80 × total paid). If the pool value of circulating BNAGENT is below the line, the mint burns BNAGENT from the pool, raising the price by at most 0.8% per transaction and always keeping at least 116,666,667 BNAGENT in the pool.
  • Collected tax tokens are converted to BNB for the treasury at most once per block and at most 0.10% of the pool's BNAGENT reserve per conversion.
  • Minting ends at share 30,000 or at the supply cap.

5. The 30-day rule

The clock starts when minting is first opened. If 10,000 shares (counting the 1,100 burned) are not reached within 30 days, the team can withdraw the parked 80% of all payments to its treasury. It can do this as many times as it wants, and minting continues after each withdrawal.

  • Only the treasury address can withdraw, only after the 30 days, and never once the pool has opened. Before the deadline nobody can withdraw anything.
  • Each withdrawal takes all USDT parked in the mint contract so far. The BNAGENT parked for the pool with those payments (40,000,000 per share) is sent to the dead address, so it can never be used or sold.
  • Minting is not stopped. People who mint after a withdrawal pay the same prices and get the same 50,000,000 BNAGENT per share, but the team can withdraw their parked 80% too, later, as long as share 10,000 has not been reached.
  • If share 10,000 is reached later, the pool opens with only the USDT and BNAGENT parked after the team's last withdrawal. Example: if the team withdraws at share 8,000 and the pool opens at 10,000, the pool starts with 2,000 × 0.80 = 1,600 USDT instead of 7,120 USDT. A smaller pool means lower liquidity and larger price swings for everyone.
  • Tokens stay non-transferable until the pool opens. If it never opens, they never become transferable. Nothing is refunded to minters.
  • There is no other way to take USDT out of the mint contract. Every withdrawal is public on-chain (event UnfilledWithdrawn, and valve() shows the count and total withdrawn).

6. Admin controls

Until it calls renounce(), the treasury can (a) pause / resume minting and (b) block / unblock a contract that acts as a BNAGENT trading pool. renounce() removes both powers permanently. The 30-day withdrawal (repeatable, see section 5) is tied to the treasury address, not to these powers, so it still works after renounce(). Nobody can mint outside the price table, change the tax, change its recipient, freeze a wallet or remove the pool's liquidity. Minting starts paused and is opened by the treasury after deployment.

1. BNAGENT 是什么

BNAGENT 是 BNB Smart Chain 上的 meme 代币，采用链上公平铸造，只对持有 .ai Agent ID 的币安 Agentic Wallet 开放，机制沿用 $JAGENT（jager.live）。代币只在份额被铸造时产生，无预售、无私募、无团队份额。

2. 代币

  • 名称/符号：BNAGENT / BNAGENT，18 位小数，BEP-20。
  • 硬上限：4,440,000,000,000 枚，由代币合约强制执行，只有铸造合约可以增发。
  • 买入税 1%（换成 BNB 给金库）；卖出税 1% 销毁 + 1% 换成 BNB 给金库；钱包间转账免税。税率与接收方写死在代码中。
  • 开池之前 BNAGENT 不能转账。铸出第 10,000 份的那笔交易会自动开池，并在同一笔交易中解除转账锁定。之后只认官方 BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap 交易对：转入其他持有 BNAGENT 的交易对、PancakeSwap V4 PoolManager 和 Infinity Vault 会被拒绝；路由合约永远不能被封。

3. 铸造

  • 共 30,000 份。第 #1–#1,100 份在部署时销毁：永不出售，也没有对应的代币或 USDT。从第 #1,101 份开始发售，可售 28,900 份（按价格表合计 46,300 USDT）。
  • 资格：发送交易的钱包必须持有 .ai Agent ID（链上读取 SPACE ID 注册表）。
  • 额度：每个 Agent ID 10 份；被邀请的 Agent ID 首次付费铸造，邀请人 +1；每完成一项社交任务 +1（共 3 项）。每笔最多 10 份。
  • 第 #1,101–#10,000 份每份 1 USDT，得 50,000,000 BNAGENT。第 10,001 份起按池价 9.9 折买入。
  • 可用 USDT（先授权，再带最高价铸造）或 BNB 支付（按 Chainlink BNB/USD 价格 +1% 计价，同一笔交易内在 PancakeSwap 换成 USDT，多付的 BNB 退回）。
  • 每笔付款 20% 直接进入项目金库（0x49E6…9BCE），80% 留在铸造合约用于流动性。BNB 付款兑换所得超出价格表的部分也进金库。

4. 流动性

  • 第 10,000 份之前，每份在铸造合约中存放 0.80 USDT 和 40,000,000 BNAGENT。达到第 10,000 份的那笔交易用全部存放的资产自动在 PancakeSwap 建立官方 BNAGENT/USDT 池：8,900 份付费份额 × 0.80 = 7,120 USDT，配 356,000,000,000 BNAGENT（同样是 1 USDT = 50,000,000 BNAGENT）——如果团队按 30 天规则提取过，则只有其最后一次提取之后留存的部分（见第 5 节）。LP 发往黑洞地址销毁，任何人都无法撤出这部分流动性。
  • 此后每笔铸造款的 80% 按池价加入资金池，LP 同样销毁。
  • 地板线：线 = 累计付款 USDT × 83,492 / 46,300（约为累计付款的 1.80 倍）。流通 BNAGENT 按池价计算的市值低于该线时，铸造会从池中销毁 BNAGENT 抬高价格，每笔最多抬升 0.8%，池中始终保留至少 116,666,667 枚。
  • 收到的税币每个区块最多兑换一次，每次不超过池中 BNAGENT 储备的 0.10%，换成 BNB 发往金库。
  • 铸满 30,000 份或达到供应上限即结束。

5. 30 天规则

从首次开放铸造开始计时。若 30 天内未达到第 10,000 份（含已销毁的 1,100 份），团队可以把全部付款中留存的 80% 提取到金库。团队可以提取任意多次，每次提取后铸造继续进行。

  • 只有金库地址能提取，只能在 30 天之后，一旦开池就永远不能再提取；期限之前任何人都不能提取。
  • 每次提取会拿走铸造合约中目前留存的全部 USDT。随这些付款为资金池存放的 BNAGENT（每份 40,000,000）会发往黑洞地址，永远无法使用或出售。
  • 铸造不会停止。提取之后铸造的人支付同样的价格、每份同样得到 50,000,000 BNAGENT，但只要还没到第 10,000 份，团队之后同样可以提取他们留存的 80%。
  • 如果之后达到第 10,000 份，资金池只用团队最后一次提取之后留存的 USDT 和 BNAGENT 开池。例如：团队在第 8,000 份时提取、在第 10,000 份开池，池子开始时只有 2,000 × 0.80 = 1,600 USDT，而不是 7,120 USDT。池子越小，流动性越低，价格波动越大。
  • 开池之前代币不可转账；如果永远不开池，代币就永远不可转账。不会向铸造者退款。
  • 除此之外没有任何从铸造合约取出 USDT 的途径。每次提取都在链上公开（事件 UnfilledWithdrawn，valve() 显示提取次数和累计金额）。

6. 管理权限

在调用 renounce() 之前，金库可以 (a) 暂停/恢复铸造，(b) 封禁/解封充当 BNAGENT 交易池的合约。renounce() 会永久移除这两项权限。30 天提取权（可多次，见第 5 节）绑定金库地址而不是这些权限，因此 renounce() 之后仍然有效。任何人都不能在价格表之外铸造、修改税率或接收方、冻结钱包或撤出池子的流动性。铸造在部署时处于暂停状态，由金库开启。
