Whitepaper白皮书

1. What BNAGENT is

BNAGENT is a meme token on BNB Smart Chain with a fair, on-chain mint reserved for Binance Agentic Wallets holding a .ai Agent ID. Supply only comes into existence when shares are minted. There is no presale and no private round. 100% of mint proceeds go to the project treasury; the only tokens the treasury receives are the 400,000,000,000 BNAGENT minted with share 10,000 for the liquidity the team adds manually.

2. Token

  • Name / symbol: BNAGENT / BNAGENT, 18 decimals, BEP-20.
  • Hard cap: 4,440,000,000,000 BNAGENT, enforced by the token. Only the mint contract can create tokens.
  • Buy tax 1% (converted to BNB for the treasury). Sell tax 1% burned + 1% converted to BNB for the treasury. Wallet-to-wallet transfers: no tax. Rates and recipient are fixed in code.
  • Until the treasury calls openTrading(), BNAGENT cannot be transferred (the only exception: the treasury moving tokens into the official pair to add liquidity). Afterwards only the official BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap pair is accepted as a pool: transfers into other pairs holding BNAGENT, the PancakeSwap V4 PoolManager and the Infinity Vault are refused. Routers can never be blocked.

3. Minting

  • Eligibility: the sending wallet must hold a .ai Agent ID (read on-chain from the SPACE ID registry).
  • Quota: 10 shares per Agent ID, +1 per invited Agent ID's first paid mint, +1 per social task (3 tasks). Max 10 shares per transaction.
  • Shares 1–10,000 give 50,000,000 BNAGENT each. Share 10,001 and later can be minted only after trading opens; the payment then buys BNAGENT at the pool price minus 1%.
  • Pay with USDT (approve, then mint with a maximum cost) or with BNB (priced with Chainlink BNB/USD plus 1%, swapped to USDT on PancakeSwap in the same transaction; BNB above the quote is refunded).
  • 100% of each payment goes straight to the project treasury (0x49E6…9BCE) in the same transaction, in USDT (BNB payments are swapped to USDT first, and the whole swap output goes to the treasury). The mint contract holds no funds.

4. Liquidity

  • The official BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap pair is created empty at deployment. No contract adds, locks or burns liquidity.
  • The transaction that mints share 10,000 also mints 400,000,000,000 BNAGENT to the treasury. The team then adds liquidity manually by pairing these tokens with USDT from the treasury. The amount of USDT, and so the opening price, is the team's decision. The LP tokens stay with the treasury: they are not locked or burned, and the team can remove that liquidity.
  • Minting pauses at share 10,000 until the treasury calls openTrading(). This works only after share 10,000 and once the official pair holds liquidity, and it can never be undone.
  • Floor line (active only after trading opens): line = total USDT paid × 85,477 / 47,400 (≈ 1.80 × total paid). If the pool value of circulating BNAGENT is below the line, the mint burns BNAGENT from the pool, raising the price by at most 0.8% per transaction and always keeping at least 116,666,667 BNAGENT in the pool.
  • Collected tax tokens are converted to BNB for the treasury at most once per block and at most 0.10% of the pool's BNAGENT reserve per conversion.
  • Minting ends at share 30,000 or at the supply cap.

5. Admin controls

The treasury calls openTrading() once (see section 4). Until it calls renounce() it can also (a) pause / resume minting and (b) block / unblock a contract that acts as a BNAGENT trading pool. renounce() removes both powers permanently. The treasury holds all mint proceeds and the LP, and adds liquidity manually. Nobody can mint outside the price table, change the tax, change its recipient or freeze a wallet. Minting starts paused and is opened by the treasury after deployment.

6. Independence

BNAGENT is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Binance, SPACE ID, PancakeSwap or Chainlink; it only uses their public on-chain contracts.

1. BNAGENT 是什么

BNAGENT 是 BNB Smart Chain 上的 meme 代币，采用链上公平铸造，只对持有 .ai Agent ID 的币安 Agentic Wallet 开放。代币只在份额被铸造时产生，无预售、无私募。铸造款 100% 进入项目金库；金库得到的代币只有第 10,000 份时铸给它的 400,000,000,000 枚，用于团队手动添加流动性。

2. 代币

  • 名称/符号：BNAGENT / BNAGENT，18 位小数，BEP-20。
  • 硬上限：4,440,000,000,000 枚，由代币合约强制执行，只有铸造合约可以增发。
  • 买入税 1%（换成 BNB 给金库）；卖出税 1% 销毁 + 1% 换成 BNB 给金库；钱包间转账免税。税率与接收方写死在代码中。
  • 在金库调用 openTrading() 之前，BNAGENT 不能转账（唯一例外：金库把代币转入官方交易对以添加流动性）。开放交易后只认官方 BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap 交易对：转入其他持有 BNAGENT 的交易对、PancakeSwap V4 PoolManager 和 Infinity Vault 会被拒绝；路由合约永远不能被封。

3. 铸造

  • 资格：发送交易的钱包必须持有 .ai Agent ID（链上读取 SPACE ID 注册表）。
  • 额度：每个 Agent ID 10 份；被邀请的 Agent ID 首次付费铸造，邀请人 +1；每完成一项社交任务 +1（共 3 项）。每笔最多 10 份。
  • 第 1–10,000 份每份得 50,000,000 BNAGENT。第 10,001 份起只有在开放交易后才能铸造，按池价 9.9 折买入。
  • 可用 USDT（先授权，再带最高价铸造）或 BNB 支付（按 Chainlink BNB/USD 价格 +1% 计价，同一笔交易内在 PancakeSwap 换成 USDT，多付的 BNB 退回）。
  • 每笔付款 100% 在同一笔交易内以 USDT 直接进入项目金库（0x49E6…9BCE）（BNB 付款先换成 USDT，兑换所得全部进金库）。铸造合约不留存任何资金。

4. 流动性

  • 官方 BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap 交易对在部署时创建（空池）。没有任何合约会自动加池、锁定或销毁 LP。
  • 铸出第 10,000 份的那笔交易同时向金库铸造 400,000,000,000 BNAGENT。随后由团队手动用金库中的 USDT 与这些代币配对添加流动性，投入多少 USDT（即开盘价）由团队决定。LP 由金库持有，不锁定、不销毁，团队可以撤出这部分流动性。
  • 铸造在第 10,000 份后暂停，直到金库调用 openTrading() 开放交易：必须已售出第 10,000 份且官方交易对已有流动性，开放后不可撤销。
  • 地板线（仅在开放交易后生效）：线 = 累计付款 USDT × 85,477 / 47,400（约为累计付款的 1.80 倍）。流通 BNAGENT 按池价计算的市值低于该线时，铸造会从池中销毁 BNAGENT 抬高价格，每笔最多抬升 0.8%，池中始终保留至少 116,666,667 枚。
  • 收到的税币每个区块最多兑换一次，每次不超过池中 BNAGENT 储备的 0.10%，换成 BNB 发往金库。
  • 铸满 30,000 份或达到供应上限即结束。

5. 管理权限

金库可调用一次 openTrading()（见第 4 节）。在调用 renounce() 之前，还可以 (a) 暂停/恢复铸造，(b) 封禁/解封充当 BNAGENT 交易池的合约。renounce() 会永久移除这两项权限。铸造款与 LP 均由金库持有，流动性由团队手动添加。任何人都不能在价格表之外铸造、修改税率或接收方、冻结钱包。铸造在部署时处于暂停状态，由金库开启。
