Whitepaper白皮书

1. What BNAGENT is

BNAGENT is a meme token on BNB Smart Chain with a fair, on-chain mint reserved for Binance Agentic Wallets holding a .ai Agent ID. Supply only comes into existence when shares are minted. There is no presale, no private round and no team allocation.

2. Token

  • Name / symbol: BNAGENT / BNAGENT, 18 decimals, BEP-20.
  • Hard cap: 4,440,000,000,000 BNAGENT, enforced by the token. Only the mint contract can create tokens.
  • Buy tax 1% (converted to BNB for the treasury). Sell tax 1% burned + 1% converted to BNB for the treasury. Wallet-to-wallet transfers: no tax. Rates and recipient are fixed in code.
  • Before the pool opens, BNAGENT cannot be transferred at all. Afterwards only the official BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap pair is accepted as a pool: transfers into other pairs holding BNAGENT, the PancakeSwap V4 PoolManager and the Infinity Vault are refused. Routers can never be blocked.

3. Minting

  • Eligibility: the sending wallet must hold a .ai Agent ID (read on-chain from the SPACE ID registry).
  • Quota: 10 shares per Agent ID, +1 per invited Agent ID's first paid mint, +1 per social task (3 tasks). Max 10 shares per transaction.
  • Shares 1–10,000 give 50,000,000 BNAGENT each. After the pool opens, the payment buys BNAGENT at the pool price minus 1%.
  • Pay with USDT (approve, then mint with a maximum cost) or with BNB (priced with Chainlink BNB/USD plus 1%, swapped to USDT on PancakeSwap; BNB above the quote is refunded, USDT above the cost goes to the treasury).
  • 80% of each payment goes to liquidity, 20% to the treasury (0x49E6…9BCE).

4. Liquidity

  • The transaction that mints share 10,000 adds 8,000 USDT and 400,000,000,000 BNAGENT to the official pair and sends the LP tokens to 0x…dEaD. Trading opens in the same transaction, at the same price minters paid (1 USDT per 50,000,000 BNAGENT).
  • After that, each mint pairs 80% of its payment with BNAGENT at the pool price; new LP also goes to the dead address.
  • Floor line: line = total USDT paid × 85,477 / 47,400 (≈ 1.80 × total paid). If the pool value of circulating BNAGENT is below the line, the mint burns BNAGENT from the pool, raising the price by at most 0.8% per transaction and always keeping at least 116,666,667 BNAGENT in the pool.
  • Collected tax tokens are converted to BNB for the treasury at most once per block and at most 0.10% of the pool's BNAGENT reserve per conversion.
  • Minting ends at share 30,000 or at the supply cap.

5. Admin controls

Until the treasury calls renounce() it can only (a) pause / resume minting and (b) block / unblock a contract that acts as a BNAGENT trading pool. renounce() removes both powers permanently. Nobody can mint outside the price table, change the tax, change its recipient or freeze a wallet. Minting starts paused and is opened by the treasury after deployment.

6. Independence

BNAGENT is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Binance, SPACE ID, PancakeSwap or Chainlink; it only uses their public on-chain contracts.

1. BNAGENT 是什么

BNAGENT 是 BNB Smart Chain 上的 meme 代币，采用链上公平铸造，只对持有 .ai Agent ID 的币安 Agentic Wallet 开放。代币只在份额被铸造时产生，无预售、无私募、无团队份额。

2. 代币

  • 名称/符号：BNAGENT / BNAGENT，18 位小数，BEP-20。
  • 硬上限：4,440,000,000,000 枚，由代币合约强制执行，只有铸造合约可以增发。
  • 买入税 1%（换成 BNB 给金库）；卖出税 1% 销毁 + 1% 换成 BNB 给金库；钱包间转账免税。税率与接收方写死在代码中。
  • 开池前 BNAGENT 完全不能转账。开池后只认官方 BNAGENT/USDT PancakeSwap 交易对：转入其他持有 BNAGENT 的交易对、PancakeSwap V4 PoolManager 和 Infinity Vault 会被拒绝；路由合约永远不能被封。

3. 铸造

  • 资格：发送交易的钱包必须持有 .ai Agent ID（链上读取 SPACE ID 注册表）。
  • 额度：每个 Agent ID 10 份；被邀请的 Agent ID 首次付费铸造，邀请人 +1；每完成一项社交任务 +1（共 3 项）。每笔最多 10 份。
  • 第 1–10,000 份每份得 50,000,000 BNAGENT；开池后按池价 9.9 折买入。
  • 可用 USDT（先授权，再带最高价铸造）或 BNB 支付（按 Chainlink BNB/USD 价格 +1% 计价并在 PancakeSwap 换成 USDT；多付的 BNB 退回，换出超过成本的 USDT 归金库）。
  • 每笔付款 80% 进流动性，20% 进金库（0x49E6…9BCE）。

4. 流动性

  • 铸出第 10,000 份的那笔交易会向官方交易对加入 8,000 USDT 和 400,000,000,000 BNAGENT，LP 打入 0x…dEaD，同一笔交易内开放交易，开盘价与铸造价一致（1 USDT = 50,000,000 BNAGENT）。
  • 之后每次铸造把付款的 80% 按池价配对加入池子，新 LP 同样打入黑洞。
  • 地板线：线 = 累计付款 USDT × 85,477 / 47,400（约为累计付款的 1.80 倍）。流通 BNAGENT 按池价计算的市值低于该线时，铸造会从池中销毁 BNAGENT 抬高价格，每笔最多抬升 0.8%，池中始终保留至少 116,666,667 枚。
  • 收到的税币每个区块最多兑换一次，每次不超过池中 BNAGENT 储备的 0.10%，换成 BNB 发往金库。
  • 铸满 30,000 份或达到供应上限即结束。

5. 管理权限

在金库调用 renounce() 之前，只能 (a) 暂停/恢复铸造，(b) 封禁/解封充当 BNAGENT 交易池的合约。renounce() 会永久移除这两项权限。任何人都不能在价格表之外铸造、修改税率或接收方、冻结钱包。铸造在部署时处于暂停状态，由金库开启。
